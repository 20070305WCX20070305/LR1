# 量子引力

1. 传统的量子力学不能很好的反应“时空与物质的相互作用”
2. 广义相对论的致命缺陷：不能处理黑洞这种极端状态

**把时空看做量子粒子的集合则可以从全新的角度解释黑洞**

1. 成就：They’ve found that **entanglement between particles gives space-time its structure**, building an environment where matter can move — and satisfying the conditions of Wheeler’s first statement. 
2. 不足：But the origin of Wheeler’s second statement remained mysterious; in their models, matter didn’t tell space how to curve. 也就是说物质如何弯曲时空的描述是缺失的

现在的研究：不足之处被描述出来了recently determined how quantum particles could give space-time its bendiness. It’s a measure of quantumness called “magic.” 

magic是一种衡量量子系统“非经典性”和“复杂程度”的严格数学指标。“魔法态”

量子引力——与爱因斯坦弯曲时空并列的理解宇宙的方式

1. 黑洞：球状粒子的集合
2. they described an exotic, static world（奇异的静态的，比如黑洞） as a throng of interacting particles, also arranged in a sphere.
3. replace the 3D region of space-time with particles on the region’s surface.相当于微分方程的边界条件、黑洞的熵
是由于黑洞的表面积决定的（一个典型的例子）
4. Entanglement, a quantum property that links particles to one another, seems to serve as space’s connective tissue.量子纠缠与连接宇宙中的点，形成三维空间
